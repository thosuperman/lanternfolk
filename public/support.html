Support & Contact

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · applies to Lanternfolk (lanternfolk.hayaix.tech) only · English

Contact

For help, feedback, bug reports or privacy questions, email hello@hayaix.tech. Please mention Lanternfolk and, if relevant, your headset model and Quest Browser version. Lanternfolk is a free personal project, so replies may take a few days.

Quick fixes

"Immersive VR is not available here"

Open the page in the Meta Quest Browser on a Meta Quest headset. Desktop and phone browsers show a preview only.

My hands are not detected

Put your controllers down and wait a moment. Make sure hand tracking is turned on in your headset's settings (on Meta Quest it is under the movement and hand-tracking settings). Good, even lighting helps tracking.

The grove is in an awkward place

Sit comfortably, look straight ahead and use your headset's recenter gesture or button. The grove moves to about an arm's length in front of you.

A spirit will not cross my hand

Hold your hand flat and still across the gap, at about the height of the path. A loose, open hand works best. If your hand tilts a lot, moves suddenly or leaves, the spirit floats back to safety and waits for you to try again. Moving slowly and keeping your palm level always works.

I can't hear anything

Check the headset volume, then touch the small moon-bell beside the grove: it toggles the music. Sound starts after you press Enter VR.

Known limitations

  • Lanternfolk was developed and tested in the Immersive Web Emulator. It has not yet been tested by the developer on a physical Meta Quest headset, so hand-tracking feel and performance on real hardware may differ.
  • Hand tracking is required. Controllers may be visible, but the puzzles are designed for hands.
  • There is no save system: each visit is one complete playthrough of the three chapters.
