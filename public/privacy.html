Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · applies to Lanternfolk (lanternfolk.hayaix.tech) only · English

In short: Lanternfolk has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies. The game does not store anything on your device and does not send anything about you anywhere. Your hand-tracking data is used only in your headset's memory, frame by frame, to run the game, and it is never saved or transmitted. Like every website, the hosting provider processes basic connection data (such as your IP address) to deliver the files.

1. Who is responsible

Lanternfolk (lanternfolk.hayaix.tech) is a free personal project by Tho Nguyen ("I", "me"), an independent developer in Japan. Contact: hello@hayaix.tech. This policy covers Lanternfolk only. The HAYAiX showcase and other projects have their own documents.

2. Hand tracking and headset data

  • When you enter VR, the game asks your browser for WebXR hand tracking. If you allow it, your browser provides the positions of 25 points (joints) on each hand, many times per second.
  • Lanternfolk uses these positions only to run the gameplay on your device: detecting a touch, a pinch, or an open palm the spirits can walk on. The joint data is kept in memory for the current frame only. It is never stored, recorded, uploaded or shared.
  • The game also uses the headset's position and direction to place the grove in front of you. This data is also used in memory only.
  • Lanternfolk does not use the camera, passthrough images, microphone, eye tracking, location or contacts.

3. What is not collected

There are no user accounts, forms, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, crash reporting or fingerprinting. The game does not set cookies and does not write to local storage, session storage or IndexedDB. (The Immersive Web SDK contains code that can save spatial-anchor IDs in local storage, but that feature is switched off in Lanternfolk.) Your browser may keep its normal cache of the site's files, which you can clear in the browser settings.

4. Hosting

The site is static files hosted on Amazon Web Services (Amazon S3 with Amazon CloudFront). To deliver the files, AWS necessarily processes connection data such as your IP address, browser user agent and the requested URL, as described in the AWS Privacy Notice. I have not enabled access logging on the Lanternfolk CloudFront distribution or S3 bucket, so I do not receive logs of individual visits.

5. Third-party requests

In my testing, the game loads files only from lanternfolk.hayaix.tech. The Immersive Web SDK contains code that can download 3D controller models from the public jsDelivr CDN for some controller types. I did not observe this request in testing, but if it happens, jsDelivr receives your IP address and browser details.

6. Email

If you email me, I use your email address and message only to reply and to fix problems you report. I keep them as long as needed for that purpose and delete them on request.

7. Children

Lanternfolk contains no violence and no user-generated content, and it collects no personal data. Meta Quest headsets and accounts have their own age requirements, set by Meta. Please follow them and use VR under adult supervision where required.

8. Your rights

Because the game collects no personal data, there is normally nothing to access or delete. For any privacy question or request, including under Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information or the laws of your country, email hello@hayaix.tech.

9. Platform

Your headset, Meta account and the Meta Quest Browser are provided by Meta under Meta's own terms and privacy policy. Lanternfolk does not receive data from your Meta account.

10. Changes

If the way Lanternfolk handles data changes, I will update this page and its "Last updated" date before the change goes live.
