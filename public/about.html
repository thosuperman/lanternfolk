About Lanternfolk

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · applies to Lanternfolk (lanternfolk.hayaix.tech) only · English

Lanternfolk is a short, peaceful VR game about helping tiny lost lantern spirits find their way home, using your own hands.

The idea

A miniature moonlit grove floats in front of you. Little spirits are asleep on islands that have drifted apart. Wake them with a touch, hold your palm across a gap so they can walk over your hand, let one climb onto your palm and lift it up a cliff, and lay a glowing leaf as a bridge for two friends. Every spirit you bring home makes the grove a little warmer: lanterns light up, flowers bloom and the music grows. At the end the spirits gather beneath the great tree, and the first friend you helped comes over to say thank you.

The core idea: your hands can become someone else's way home. Lanternfolk is designed to be calm and comforting. It is a relaxing entertainment experience, not a medical, therapeutic or wellness treatment, and it makes no health claims.

How to play

  • Open lanternfolk.hayaix.tech in the Meta Quest Browser and choose Enter VR.
  • Put your controllers down. Lanternfolk is played entirely with hand tracking: touch, an open palm, and a pinch.
  • Play seated. Everything is within a comfortable reach in front of you. A full playthrough takes about 3–5 minutes.
  • Touch the small moon-bell beside the grove to turn the music on or off.

Built with

  • WebXR with the Hand Input module, using Meta's open-source Immersive Web SDK (IWSDK) and Three.js.
  • All 3D scenery, characters, music and sound effects are generated by the game's own code. There are no downloaded art or audio assets.
  • Developed and tested with the Immersive Web Emulator. See Support for known limitations.

Who made it

Lanternfolk is a personal project by Tho Nguyen, an independent developer in Japan, and is part of the HAYAiX project showcase. It was created in November 2026 as an entry to the Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026 (Gaming track). It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta.
